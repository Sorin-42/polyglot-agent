Polyglot Agent for Mac

1. Drag "Polyglot Agent" onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open your Applications folder and double-click "Polyglot Agent".

The first time, macOS warns that it can't check the app for malicious software,
because the app isn't registered with Apple. To open it anyway (only needed once):

  macOS 15 (Sequoia) or newer
   a. In the warning, click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").
   b. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
   c. Scroll down to the message about "Polyglot Agent" and click "Open Anyway".
   d. Enter your Mac password, then click "Open Anyway" once more.

  macOS 14 (Sonoma) or older
   a. In Applications, hold Control and click "Polyglot Agent", then choose "Open".
   b. Click "Open" in the message that appears.

After that, it opens with a normal double-click, in its own window.

On first start the app asks where to keep your data (memory, chats, projects and
settings) and which language you use. Then open Settings (the gear, top right) and
paste your Anthropic API key from https://platform.claude.com

To quit: close the window, or press Command-Q.
